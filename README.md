A procedural newspaper front page summing up 18 November 2026 in a single 1024x1024 image, drawn entirely by code — no photographs, no downloads, no randomness. The lead story is Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meeting in Shenzhen ahead of the first-ever trilateral summit with Donald Trump, followed by Iran's strikes on US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, the partial collapse of the Kerch bridge, Mary Peltola's Alaska Senate win, the United States losing its measles-elimination status, Nvidia's $115.8B quarter, and the Quad leaders meeting in Manila, with a ticker carrying the Ebola toll in DR Congo, the cold threatening displaced families in Gaza, the Siberian plane crash, and Voyager 1's light-day milestone. Every headline, pictogram, and rule line is produced by a hand-rolled raster canvas, a small 5x7 bitmap font, and a hand-written PNG encoder in a single TypeScript file.

Run it with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
